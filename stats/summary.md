## Notes & caveats

- **Readme headline counts are stale for this snapshot.** The upstream readme (kept verbatim in
  `readme.md`) claims *744,757 lines / 5,464,818 words*; the pinned snapshot (upstream commit
  `04e0778`, 2026-03-05, per `PROVENANCE.md`) actually contains **754,726 sentences and 5,688,416
  annotated word rows** (both re-verified independently of the generator script:
  `grep -h -r '^# sent_id' files | wc -l` and a null-delimited `awk` pass over all 15,900 files).
- The observed **5,688,416** word count exactly matches the token count of the DCS master
  (`dcs full.sqlite`) recorded in the VisualDCS concordance work (H5153), corroborating that this
  mirror is complete for the non-commentary dump.
- **XPOS is empty (`_`) throughout this dump** — only the auto-mapped UPOS column is populated.
  The language-specific tag set described in the upstream readme is not present in these files.
- **Junction definition**: a "sandhi junction" is an internal word boundary inside a multiword
  (range) token, i.e. where the DCS annotation itself sandhi-univerbated ≥2 words into one surface
  string. Boundaries between separate surface strings (space-separated pada) are not counted.
  `left_end`/`right_start` characters are taken from the `Unsandhied` MISC field (falling back to
  FORM), so they reflect the padapāṭha-shaped segmented forms, not the fused surface.
- `UnsandhiedReconstructed` (62.1% of words) flags unsandhied forms produced by reconstruction
  rather than the annotator; `Unsandhied` itself covers 100% of word rows.
- Frequency dictionary is lemma-level (lemma × UPOS). Form-level and sense-level (WordSem)
  frequency tables are natural follow-ups; `word-senses.csv` in `lookup/` supports the latter.
